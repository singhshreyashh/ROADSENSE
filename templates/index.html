🎯 RoadSense Hackathon Presentation Guide

Follow this 4-step walkthrough for an impactful, high-score hackathon presentation:

Step 1: Commuter Experience (Driver Portal)

Switch to the Driver Sensing Portal. Point out the live simulated accelerometer HUD streaming at 10Hz and the privacy notice. Click ⚡ Inject Pothole Bump to simulate driving over a hazard. Note the instant shockwave on the canvas and notification!

Step 2: Crowdsource Corroboration & Confidence Boosting

Click + Report Road Hazard to submit a manual citizen report on MG Road. The clustering algorithm identifies the spatial proximity, merges the report into the cluster, and boosts confidence from 40% to 94%! Explain how multiple trips turn sensor noise into verified truth.

Step 3: Municipal Operations & Interactive Leaflet Map

Switch to the Public Works Dashboard. Show the interactive Leaflet map with custom icons (craters for potholes, stop signs for harsh braking, curves for swerves) and cluster badges (e.g. 5x). Filter by Critical severity or switch the basemap tile provider.

Step 4: Evidence Inspection & Repair Lifecycle

Click Inspect on #RS-101. Open the drawer to reveal the sensor waveform impulse chart and multi-trip timeline. Click through the 5-stage repair pipeline: Reported → Scheduled → Resolved to close the loop!
